How to Prepare for a Professional Headshot in Macon, GA

A headshot for LinkedIn has a different job from a portrait on the front page of your business website. Decide where the image will appear before you choose what to wear or where to take it. That one decision makes the rest of the session easier to plan.

Start with where the photo will appear

For a LinkedIn photo, a tighter crop keeps your face easy to recognize in a small profile circle. A website bio might leave more room for your shoulders or the setting. If the image will sit beside text, mention which side of the layout needs extra space.

Choose clothes you can move in

Bring one familiar outfit and one backup with a different level of formality. Try both while sitting and standing, and check that the collar or jacket sits comfortably. If the photo will be cropped close, pay particular attention to what’s visible around your face.

Choose a setting that fits your work

A studio background can keep the focus on your expression. An office or workspace can give a branding portrait more context. Ashah Photography offers studio and on-location sessions. Browse headshots and branding photography in Macon to compare styles before deciding what feels right for you.

Give yourself time to settle in

Start with a small turn of your shoulders, let out a breath, and think of how you’d greet someone you know. If you’d like to know who’s behind the camera, read Ashah Smith’s story and learn about her approach to guided portrait sessions.

Bring a screenshot of the page where the photo will live. It gives your photographer a reference for the crop, background and mood.

Before your session

  • Decide where you’ll use the headshot.
  • Pack a second outfit that still feels like you.
  • Bring a reference for the crop or background.
